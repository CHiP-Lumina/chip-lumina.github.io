We participated in the [**Dreiländertagung der Universitäts- und Wissenschaftsarchive**](https://cesarch.cz/anmeldung-zur-tagung/) (Fourth Czech-German-Austrian Conference of University and Scientific Archives), held on March 16–18, 2026 at the Karolinum in Prague.

The conference brought together archivists from higher education institutions and research facilities across the Czech Republic, Germany, and Austria.

We presented our work with a poster and a talk, introducing the CHiP Lumina project to the archival community.
